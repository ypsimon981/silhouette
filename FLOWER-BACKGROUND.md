# Dark grass background — v0.22.57

Only the Flowers level uses the new deep-green photographic grass background. The grass is fine, muted and evenly lit; a pale sage rim keeps the existing target contours legible on the dark ground. The original paths, silhouettes, reveal images, input, placement tolerance, timing, audio, scores and shadows are unchanged. Potato linen and animal footprints retain their approved appearance.

Production asset: assets/backgrounds/grass-dark.webp, 512 × 512 RGB, 220,088 bytes, lossless WebP. The generated photograph is addressed with a 2 × 2 mirrored tile before encoding, so opposite borders are exactly identical. It repeats at a fixed 256 × 256 CSS pixels; it is not stretched to cover the board. There is no live Canvas, filter, frame loop or animation in this background. CSS provides a dark-green fallback while the image loads. The mode-selection token invalidates pending backgrounds from earlier levels.

Built-in image generation prompt:
> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Production seamless square game background texture of dark green short lawn grass seen EXACTLY from above, orthographic, edge-to-edge grass only. Dense small fine grass blades in gently varying directions, natural photographic fine material detail. Deep muted forest-green and subdued moss-green, low saturation, LOW CONTRAST, evenly shaded with diffuse overhead light. Small-scale blades, about 70 blades across width, no large leaves, no tall clumps, no flowers, no dirt patches, no bare ground, no dew or bright specular highlights. No sun streaks, no strong shadows, no bright green areas, no vignette or overall light gradient, no objects, text or borders. A quiet background behind a silhouette puzzle: clearly grass, but visually calm, with fine unobtrusive detail. Opposite edges should tile seamlessly. Square 1024x1024.

Validation: real Chromium mobile-size visual inspection; all decoded opposite RGB borders match exactly; fixed repeat and no animation; original ten flower targets and their pale rim; ten actual pointer placements complete the level; animal and potato backgrounds do not receive grass; background regression tests pass without JavaScript errors.
